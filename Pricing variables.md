

| Variables  | Example  | Metrics or formula  |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Duration & group size (packs)**  | Short trip (1–2 days) \+ large group (6+ pax) → fixed costs split widely → lower price per person → fits **Economy**. • Long trip (5+ days) \+ small group (1–2 pax, e.g. a couple) → fixed costs carried by very few people, plus more nights of hotel/meals → higher price per person → fits **Luxury package** • Mixed cases (long \+ large group, or short \+ small group) land in **Premium** by default until you have booking data to tune the boundary.  | **Price per pax** \= \[(Fixed cost/day × duration) ÷ group size\] \+ (Variable cost/pax × duration)  |
| **Seasons**  Season **reflects demand** and capacity pressure on the travel calendar — off-season has spare hotel rooms and guide availability  | • Off-season, regular weekday → discount to encourage booking, e.g. **−10% to −15%** off base price. • Holiday season (school holidays, year-end, CNY/Raya) → surcharge from limited slots and high demand, e.g. **\+15% to \+25%**, with a further **\+30%+** for peak dates like CNY itself.  |  **Final price \=** Base price × Season Multiplier   Suggested starting bands: Off-peak \= 0.90 · Regular \= 1.00 · Peak/School holiday \= 1.20 · Super-peak (CNY, Raya, year-end) \= 1.35.  |
| **3)Mode of transport**  Each mode has a different cost basis and flexibility level  public transport is pooled and cheap  Private car rental is flexible but priced per day   |  Economy tier uses public transport/shared van → lower cost, less schedule flexibility . • Premium/VIP tier uses private car rental or a booked flight leg → higher cost, full flexibility.  |  **Public:** `flat fare per pax × number of transfers` **• Car rental: `per-day rate × rental days` (+ fuel/driver if not bundled) • Flight/inter-city:** `fixed ticket price × pax`  |
|  **4\) Types of activities**  Different activity types carry different direct costs (entrance fees, equipment ,specialist guiding)    |  Sightseeing/walking (e.g. street art trail) → low or no entrance fee → cheaper per activity.  Adventure (caving) or specialist (photography workshop) → higher cost from equipment/specialist guide fees.  |  `Activity cost =  Σ (entrance fee + equipment/specialist fee + allocated guide-time cost)` for each selected activity.   |
| **5\) Hotels**   **Hotel star rating** and  **nightly rate** are  usually the single  largest cost  line in the package  | **•** 3-star hotel, standard room → lower nightly rate → fits Economy. • 5-star or boutique heritage hotel → higher nightly rate, may bundle breakfast/perks → fits VIP.  |  **Hotel cost** \= nightly rate × nights × rooms needed  |
| **6\) Add-ons (optional) Online que**  Priced separately because its value depends entirely on demand at that specific attraction on that specific date  **Photographer  Multilingual tour guide**  Standard English-language guidance is assumed in the base guide rate;  other languages carry a premium due to guide scarcity and specialized training.    |  • Peak season at a popular attraction → high value, priced higher. • Off-season, no queue exists → low or waived value; consider not offering it as a paid add-on for those dates.  Half-day coverage, edited highlight reel → base rate. • Full-day coverage, full edited set/raw files → higher rate.  • English guide → included in base package cost. • Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, or Arabic-speaking guide → premium due to limited local guide availability in that language.  |   **Photographer cost** \= hourly/half-day/full-day rate × duration booked   **Multilingual guide cost** \= base guide rate × language premium multiplier  |
